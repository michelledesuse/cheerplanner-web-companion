How customers consent to receive text messages from CheerPlanner.

How opt-in is collected

CheerPlanner is a mobile app for cheer/dance parents. After signing in, a user goes to Settings → Notifications, enters their mobile number, and turns the “Send me SMS reminders” toggle ON. The toggle is OFF by default; the user must actively enable it. When enabled, the app records the consent timestamp and mobile number on the user’s account. No messages are sent unless the user opts in. Consent is not a condition of using the app.

Message types & frequency

Account reminders only: payment due dates, upcoming competitions, and travel/booking deadlines. Frequency varies with the user’s own schedule (typically a few messages per month). Message and data rates may apply.

Sample message: “CheerPlanner: Tuition of $150 for Emma is due tomorrow (Jul 4). Reply STOP to opt out.”

Opt-out

Users can reply STOP at any time, or turn the toggle OFF in Settings → Notifications. Reply HELP for help.
